THE IDEA

Three rules.
Plenty to figure out.

Each number gives you an area. The shape, placement and next move are yours to work out.

01

Find a rectangle

Draw around exactly one clue. Width × height must match its number: a 6 can be 2 × 3 or 1 × 6, if there is room.

02

Finish a line

Fill every square in a row or column to clear it. Finish two lines at once for a larger score and more breathing room.

03

Keep room to play

New clues change the board. Watch NEXT, compare shapes and leave useful empty space for the numbers still to come.

MORE THAN A PERFECT FIT

Think about the space
you leave behind.

A rectangle can fit beautifully and still block your next clue. Snitrix asks you to balance the move in front of you with the board that comes after it.

Try rotating a 6, protecting a narrow lane or setting up a row-and-column clear. Classic gives you time to compare your options.

FIND YOUR PACE

One puzzle.
Find your pace.

Start with Classic to learn the shared rules. Then try a level, a timed challenge or the daily goal.

Classic

Take your time. Beat your best.

The main, open-ended score mode. Clues arrive after moves. Clear space and keep the board useful, with no move timer.

Journey Beta

A goal for every level.

Work through individual challenges with specific objectives and move limits. Read each level’s goal before drawing.

Blitz Beta

Play against the clock.

Home opens 5 Minutes: a five-minute score challenge with clues refilled after moves. Keep the board useful while the active-play clock runs.

Daily Challenge

A fresh goal for today.

Meet a dated objective within its move budget. Read today's goal, plan your rectangles and build a streak on this browser.
